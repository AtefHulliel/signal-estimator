These are estimates. Programs usually publish Signal percentages rounded to whole numbers, so the counts here are reconstructed from those rounded figures rather than measured directly. Rates are computed from the rounded counts, and the "no Signal" counts are derived by subtraction so the parts sum to the totals. Small changes in the reported percentages can shift the rates and the ratio, so treat these as a rough guide to the size of the Signal effect, not a precise probability.
